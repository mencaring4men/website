Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Men Caring4 Men ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, discloses and protects your personal information. We are committed to handling your personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

1. Who we are

Men Caring4 Men provides 1:1 coaching, mentoring and wellbeing support for men in helping professions. This policy applies to our website and all interactions with our services.

2. What personal information we collect

We only collect personal information that is reasonably necessary to provide our services. This may include:

  • Your name, email address and phone number
  • Booking and appointment details
  • Information you choose to share during enquiries or sessions
  • Payment-related information (processed securely by our third-party payment and scheduling providers — we do not store full card details)
  • Website usage data such as your IP address and pages visited, collected via analytics and cookies

3. How we collect it

We collect personal information directly from you when you complete a booking, send us an email, fill in a form, or speak with us. Some technical information is collected automatically when you use our website. Where it is reasonable and practicable, we will collect your personal information directly from you.

4. Why we collect and use it

We use your personal information to:

  • Schedule and deliver sessions and alignment calls
  • Respond to your enquiries and communicate with you
  • Process payments and keep accurate records
  • Improve our website and services
  • Meet our legal and professional obligations

We will not use your personal information for a purpose other than the one for which it was collected unless you would reasonably expect it, you have consented, or we are permitted or required by law.

5. Disclosure to third parties

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with trusted service providers who help us operate, such as scheduling (e.g. Calendly), payment processors, email and hosting providers. These providers are only given the information they need and are required to protect it.

We may disclose information where required or authorised by law. Some providers may store data outside Australia; where this occurs we take reasonable steps to ensure your information is handled consistently with the APPs.

6. Sensitive information

Some information shared during sessions may be sensitive (for example, information about your health or wellbeing). We only collect sensitive information with your consent and where it is necessary to provide our services, and we take extra care to keep it secure and confidential.

7. Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from misuse, interference, loss, and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure. This includes secure storage, restricted access and use of reputable service providers. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Cookies and website analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to understand how visitors use the site and to improve your experience. You can disable cookies through your browser settings, though some features may not function as intended.

9. Accessing and correcting your information

You may request access to the personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct it if it is inaccurate, out of date, incomplete or misleading. We will respond within a reasonable period. In limited circumstances permitted by law we may decline a request, and we will explain why in writing.

10. Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as it is needed for the purposes described in this policy or to meet legal, accounting or professional record-keeping obligations. When it is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to destroy or de-identify it.

11. Complaints

If you believe we have breached the Australian Privacy Principles, please contact us using the details below. We will acknowledge your complaint and aim to resolve it promptly. If you remain dissatisfied, you may contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be available on our website, with the "last updated" date shown above.
