02 Have this ready on the night

The whole ask is a floor and an hour

  • Somewhere to lie downA bit of floor, a rug, or the bed. A pillow helps.
  • Headphones if the house is loudNot essential. Better if the kids are still up.
  • A door you can shutSeventy-five minutes where nobody needs you. Tell whoever needs telling.
  • Nothing elseNo mat, no app, no gear, no reading beforehand. Turn up as you are.
Camera on or off is your call and nobody is called on to speak. If something on the night feels like too much, you slow the breath down, make the out-breath longer, and you are back in charge. That is the whole safety plan and Peter walks everyone through it before anything starts.

One thing worth doing today

Think of one bloke you work with who is in the same shape you are. The one who says he is fine in exactly the tone you use. Send him this page.

Nearly every man who ends up in a room like this got there because someone he trusted told him about it. You do not have to make it a big conversation. "Doing this Thursday, thought of you" is plenty.

There are 25 spots. It is a better night when a couple of them know each other.
