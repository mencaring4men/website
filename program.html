Men Caring4 Men

LIBERATION WORK

The men's breathwork program

Twelve weeks. A small group of men. A practical way back to yourself.

This page is here to tell you what it actually involves, so you can decide properly

01 What it is

A therapeutic group program for men, led by Peter Curtis: counsellor, breathwork facilitator, and a teacher for 25 years before that. The same small group of men, meeting for a 2-hour session each week. Each session combines breathwork, learning to read your own nervous system, and honest conversation.

Not therapy, not a lecture.

02 Who it is for

Men who have spent their working lives looking after other people, and are running on empty. Still functioning, still showing up, but flat at home, short with the people they love, and tired in a way sleep does not fix.

03 What you get
  • Twelve weeks of live 2-hour group sessionsThe same men every week, strict confidentiality.
  • A breath practice you can use in real timeAt work, in the car, before you walk in the front door.
  • A working map of your own stress responsesWhy you snap, why you go flat, and how to come back.
  • A daily 2-minute practiceSmall enough to fit inside any life.
  • A room of men carrying the same weightSo you stop doing this on your own.
04 What the twelve weeks cover

Where it goes

Awareness

  1. Safety, trust and intention

    Ground rules, and the anchor question: why am I here?

  2. Reconnecting to the body

    Getting out of your head for the first time in years.

  3. Emotional awareness

    Naming what you actually feel, without being swamped by it.

The armour

  1. The armour

    The ways you protect yourself, and whether they still work.

  2. Anger

    Feeling it fully without suppressing it or letting it run the show.

  3. What is hard to show

    Shame, and giving it some air.

Living it

  1. How you show up

    Connecting the patterns to your marriage, your kids and your workmates.

  2. Living differently

    The same question as week one, answered by a different man.

05 The shape of it

From surviving to living with intention

Four things carry a man across: taking responsibility for his own choices, being willing to be seen in discomfort instead of avoiding it, practising new ways of thinking and relating and acting, and being supported and challenged by other men.

The breath runs underneath everything. It is what makes the crossing possible, and it is the part you can practise on a Tuesday night in your own living room.

06 What it asks of you

Two hours a week. Two minutes a day. Honesty at your own pace.

Nothing is forced, you can pass on anything, and everything said in the room stays there.

07 Who leads it
Peter Curtis
Counsellor · Breathwork Facilitator

Peter Curtis taught for 25 years, the last 15 in ACT public schools, and burned out badly enough that at one point the system declared him unfit to teach. He fought his way back into the classroom, then retrained as a counsellor and breathwork facilitator, and built Men Caring4 Men so other men would not have to do what he did alone.

He has sat on both sides of this room.

Graduate Diploma in Personal Counselling · 25 years in education · Breathwork facilitation · Trauma-informed practice · Group facilitation
