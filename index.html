Meet Peter

I Know What It's Like To Hit The Wall

I spent 25 years in the classroom. The last 15 were in the ACT public system. I was once declared unfit to teach. I know what it looks like when a man in this profession hits the wall — and I know how little support exists for him in the space between "still showing up" and going "on workers' comp."

I recovered. I retrained. And now I work specifically with men in helping professions who are heading in the same direction.

I'm not a therapist selling you another diagnosis. I'm a former teacher who understands how systems can fail the individual, I have learned and developed a process to help you appreciate and understand yourself.
